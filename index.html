What is it?

Memory Palace — AKA The Loci Method

A mnemonic technique using spatial memory to organize and recall information by mentally placing items in familiar locations. It dates back to ancient Greece, traditionally attributed to the poet Simonides of Ceos, who discovered the technique after surviving a collapsed banquet hall and realizing he could recall the guests by remembering where they were seated. Later, Roman orators like Cicero and Quintilian used and taught it as a core tool for delivering long speeches from memory without notes.

How it works
01
The Palace

Choose a familiar place you can clearly visualize, like your home or a route you know well.

02
The Association

Turn each piece of information into a vivid image and place it in specific spots along that place.

03
The Visit

Mentally walk through the location to trigger each image and recall the information in order.

Pro tips
01
Be Absurd

The weirder and more exaggerated the image, the more memorable it becomes. Don't be logical.

02
Use Senses

Add smell, sound, and texture to each image. Multi-sensory encoding deepens the memory trace.

03
Revisit Often

Walk through your palace daily for the first week. Spaced repetition cements long-term retention.
